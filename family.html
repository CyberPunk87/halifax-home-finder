Set up

1. Install the free ntfy app on your phone and subscribe to the topic below, so check-ins arrive as notifications.
2. Press “Send a test notification”.
3. Make a link for each child, send it to them, and ask them to open it and choose Add to Home Screen.

On a second parent’s device, paste the same topic here so you both see the same map.
In Canada, 911 reaches police, ambulance and fire. The red button opens the phone’s dialer and alerts you.

Privacy. Anyone who learns the topic can read these messages and send fake ones, so only give the link to your children and never post it. ntfy.sh keeps messages for about 12 hours. Children see a note on their page that taps share their location. Live sharing only works while their page stays open with the screen on; a web page can’t track in the background. For all-day tracking, use Find My (iPhone) or Google Maps location sharing.
